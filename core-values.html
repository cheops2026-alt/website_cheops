Discovery

We learned many new things this season. We didn't just build a robot; we learned how to use sensors, how to code in Python, and how to make 3D models on the computer. We also learned about history and how to protect old objects. Every time we found a problem, we learned something new to fix it.

Innovation

We invented a smart 3D scanner to help archaeologists. Some old objects are in dangerous places or might break if they are moved, so our scanner takes pictures and measurements without touching them. We even made a website to show our 3D models. We kept improving our design until it worked perfectly.

Impact

We wanted our project to help real people. We visited museums and talked to experts to ask them what they thought. Based on their advice, we made our project better. Our goal is to help scientists study history safely without hurting the artifacts or themselves.

Inclusion

Our team is a place where everyone is important. We sit together and listen to everyone’s ideas, even if someone is a bit shy. If a teammate doesn't understand something, like how a gear works or how to write code, we stop and explain it. We make sure no one is left behind.

Teamwork

We worked together to solve big challenges. For example, when our first robot hub didn't work well, we didn't give up. We helped each other change it four times until we found the best one. We divide the work fairly and support each other so we can stay focused and happy.

Fun

We had a great time together! It wasn't just about work. We played games, went to museums, and celebrated everyone’s birthdays. We cheer for each other when we succeed and we always find reasons to smile. Being a team made this journey a lot of fun.
